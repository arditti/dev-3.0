Short: Group projects into Spaces

Projects can now be grouped into Spaces — named, many-to-many groups shown as collapsible dashboard headers with their own ordering, a membership multi-select in Project Settings and New Project, a space scope in the active-tasks sidebar, space-name matching in the project switcher, and space info with sibling paths in `dev3 current`. Every project keeps its own board, and with zero spaces nothing changes.
